# AGENTS.md

- Proposal slide images live in the private `propostas-slides` bucket (workspace blocks public buckets); `conteudo.apresentacao.slides` stores public-format URLs and the public page swaps them for signed URLs server-side, always looked up by token.
- Proposal visits (`proposta_visitas`) are written only by server functions in `propostas-publico.functions.ts` with the service role; one row per browser session, and preview (`?previa=1`, `?imprimir=1` or a signed-in Compass user) records nothing.
- Proposal alert e-mails (`src/lib/propostas/avisos-email*.ts`) go through the same Resend connector as the other Compass e-mails, to `propostas.avisos_emails` (empty → the sender's profile e-mail); they never throw, every attempt is logged in `integration_logs` (action `proposta_aviso_email`), `PROPOSTA_AVISOS_EMAIL_SIMULAR=1` skips the real send, and a signed-in Compass caller (preview) gets no e-mail.
- Site forms enter through `POST /api/public/mkt/conversao` (rules in `src/lib/mkt/conversao.ts`, writes in `conversao.server.ts`, service role only): the asset's public `chave` authenticates, every submit is a new immutable `mkt_conversoes` row, and the SDR card is created/updated in `leads` with the lead/contact spine linked by the existing `leads_vincula` trigger.
- `leads.sem_ia_automatica` (copied from `mkt_ativos.sem_ia_automatica`) must be honored by every automatic WhatsApp sender for new leads: AI opening, `new_lead`/`stage_change`/`no_reply` rules and FUP sequences; routing and the human SDR cadence still apply.
- New users: `createUser` sends no e-mail; the super admin edits and sends the welcome e-mail right after (or later via "Boas-vindas" in Usuários) through `sendWelcomeEmail` (draft/HTML in `src/lib/boas-vindas.ts`, Resend, logged in `integration_logs` action `boas_vindas_email`). It links to the public manual at `/manual` (`src/content/manual-compass.html`, served without login by `src/routes/manual.ts`).
- Usage logs: `useRegistroDeUso` (in the `_app` shell) records each tab session, page opened and a 5-minute "active" ping (only with the tab visible and recent interaction) into `uso_eventos` via `registrarUso` (service role; RLS on, no policies). Only the e-mails in `ACESSO_LOGS_USO` (`src/lib/uso.ts`, today just talita.sigales@grougp.com.br) can read them, at `/uso` (summaries come from the `uso_resumo`/`uso_telas`/`uso_ultimo_acesso` SQL functions, service role only). Events before the app started logging (from 23/11/2026) were reconstructed from login refreshes and recorded CRM actions, flagged `reconstruido = true` (tipos `aberto`/`acao`, deduped by `origem_id`); `aberto` never counts as active time.
- E-mail marketing segments come from `email_segmento_leads(regras)`: `regras.lista = <segment id>` makes it a pasted/uploaded list (`email_segmento_membros`, imported by `importarListaDeEmails`, parsed by `src/lib/email-marketing/lista.ts`; other rules are ignored, unsubscribes still excluded); `regras.carteiras` matches an executive's portfolio (cs_clients.comercial_responsavel_id, companies.owner_user_id or leads.rd_owner = profile name), unlike `responsaveis` (leads.assigned_to).
- Executives open deals themselves with "Novo negócio" (`criarNegocio` in `comercial-negocio.functions.ts`, dialog in `src/components/comercial/novo-negocio.tsx`): reuses the company by exact name and the contact by e-mail, same shape as `passar_lead_para_executivo`; only managers create for someone else. `deals.reuniao_em` is editable on the deal ("Reunião marcada") and the Início "Reuniões hoje" merges SDR leads, the executive's deals and Google Agenda events with external invitees.
- WhatsApp campaign filter `carteiras` resolves lead ids with `leads_da_carteira(uuid[])` (same portfolio rule as e-mail `regras.carteiras`), then applies the other filters in batches of 300 ids (`leadsDaAudiencia`).
- CS meetings: a scheduled meeting is a `cs_meetings` row with status `agendada` (any number per day) and optional `contact_id` (→ `cs_contacts`), created from the client page ("Agendar reunião") or the CS agenda (`AgendarReuniaoDialog`). `listCsAgenda` merges these with Google events; Google events can be linked to a client/contact by hand (`vincularEventoDaAgenda`, service role after an RLS read check). Editing a meeting updates its single timeline entry (matched by `metadata.meeting_id`) and keeps the owner. The CS dashboard counts only realized meetings as held.
- Client contacts (`cs_contacts`) have their own `tags text[]` (besides company tags in `cs_client_tags`), edited with `tagDoContato`; a realized masterclass meeting tags its contact too. The company page (`getCsClient`) hides contacts and "outras pessoas" without e-mail (they stay in the database; `semEmailOcultos` reports how many).
- Company merge (`/empresas-parecidas`, managers decide; others request — see below): `empresas_parecidas()` groups active companies by same CNPJ, same domain or `empresa_chave(nome)` (no accents/punctuation/legal suffixes), minus `empresas_grupos_ignorados`; `mesclar_empresas(fica, sai)` repoints every FK to `companies(id)` (discovered from pg_constraint), fills the survivor's empty fields, logs `empresas_mescladas` (incl. `sai_rd_org_id`) and deletes the merged one. The RD transform maps a merged company's `rd_org_id` to the survivor so it is not recreated.
- PDA analysts: `analistas_pda` (unique by lower(email)) is filled by the CSV import in Configurações CS (`lerAnalistasPda` → `importarAnalistasPda` → SQL `importar_analistas_pda`, managers only), which links the company by CNPJ, existing contact e-mail, unique corporate domain or unique `empresa_chave` name (else "sem empresa", linkable by hand with vinculo `manual`, never overwritten by re-imports) and flags `contacts`/`cs_contacts.analista_pda`. The company page tab "Analistas PDA" (`analistasDaEmpresa`) lists them plus `cs_contacts` flagged as analysts.
- Company review (`/empresas-parecidas`): each group gets a manager decision — merge, "São filiais" (`ligar_filiais`: sets `companies.parent_company_id` to a mother company from the group, an existing one or a new one, mirrors `cs_clients.parent_client_uid` when both are CS clients, logs `empresas_grupos_ignorados.decisao = 'filiais'` with `mae_id`/`filhas`) or "não são a mesma" (`decisao = 'diferentes'`); groups whose members already share one parent are hidden, so a new unlinked unit brings the group back. "Revisadas" lists decisions and merges; `reabrir_grupo_de_parecidas` undoes a decision (unlinks the filiais). `pareceFiliais` (src/lib/empresas-parecidas.ts) flags branch-like groups (unit codes, UF, "regional", same domain with different names).
- Company requests (`empresas_solicitacoes`): only managers merge, link filiais, mark "não são a mesma" or deactivate (`desativar_empresas`; "excluir" = `companies.ativo = false`). CS/comercial/executivo see `/empresas-parecidas` (DB check in `empresas_parecidas()`) and file a request with a mandatory reason (`pedirAcaoEmpresa`: name snapshot, bell notice to `ids_da_gestao()` via `notificar_usuarios` 'mencao'). `decidirPedidoDeEmpresa` claims the pending row (status update guarded by `status = 'pendente'`), runs the action with the manager's session, reverts to pending with `resultado.erro` on failure, and notifies the requester (bell + e-mail via `notificar`). Tab "Pedidos" (`?aba=pedidos`) for managers, "Meus pedidos" for others.
- CS task calendar ("Agenda de tarefas" in Configurações CS): `cs_settings.agenda_tarefas` (work days, hours, kanban capacity per day, per-person overrides, holidays — national ones pre-filled) plus `cs_ausencias` (absences with optional substitute, which also creates a `cs_delegations` row). Pure rules in `src/lib/cs/agenda-tarefas.ts`; `montarSemana` takes per-owner available weekdays/capacity, the kanban moves pending cards off unavailable days (`fixarSemanaCs.mover`), task dates set by saveCsTask/follow-up completion/reschedule/executive handoff go through `ajustarDataDaTarefa` (next available day at the start hour), and `cs_escalations()` skips owners absent today (`cs_ausente_hoje`).
- Deal responsible (`src/lib/comercial/responsavel.ts`): `executivo_user_id` is only set when an SDR books a meeting; RD-imported deals carry only `owner_user_id`. Anything "per executive" (pipeline filter, Visão geral, Análise, Minha fila, Início meetings) uses `responsavelDoNegocio` (executivo ?? owner) and the PostgREST filter `filtroDoResponsavel(id)`; never filter on `executivo_user_id` alone.
- "Nova proposta" on `/propostas` picks an open deal (`buscarNegociosParaProposta`, own deals first) and opens `/negocio/$id?proposta=nova`, which opens the proposal generator.
- Assistant actions (`src/lib/assistente/acoes.server.ts`): generic read-only `consultar_base`/`descrever_base` over the `TABELAS_DA_BASE` allow-list (session RLS; sensitive-looking keys stripped; columns via `assistente_colunas()`), plus `criar_negocio`, `definir_itens_negocio`, `gerar_proposta` (draft only), `criar_relatorio`, `criar_segmentacao`. Write tools refuse unless `confirmado: true` (the prompt makes the assistant summarise and ask first). They reuse the same cores as the screens: `criarNegocioNucleo`, `salvarItensNucleo`, `gerarPropostaNucleo` (+ exported zod schemas). `/relatorios?r=<id>` and `/email-marketing/segmentos?s=<id>` open a saved item.
- Assistant CRM actions (`src/lib/assistente/acoes-crm.server.ts`, tool `executar_acao_crm`): CS tasks/follow-ups, timeline, CS meetings, journey stage, owners, tags, alerts, and deal activity/stage/edit/freeze/close. Each action calls the screen's own core: server functions expose `<name>Schema` + `<name>Nucleo(context: CtxNucleo, data)` (`src/lib/nucleo.ts`), and the server function is just `Schema.parse` + `Nucleo(context, data)`. New actions follow the same pattern; deleting tasks is refused.
- WhatsApp lead-less conversations (personal mirrored numbers): RLS uses `pode_ver_conversa_wa(lead_id, account_id, assigned_user_id)` — with a lead, the usual lead + account rule; without a lead, managers, the account owner and the assignee only. On an `espelhada` account, if the lead already has its (unique) conversation on another account, inbound and echo/history messages go to a lead-less contact conversation on the personal number instead of the other account's inbox.
- CS time-slot offers (`src/lib/cs-reuniao-ofertas.functions.ts`, table `cs_reuniao_ofertas`): each offered slot is a `cs_meetings` row with status `reservada` and `oferta_id`, so it shows on the CS agenda and blocks time. The client picks at the public `/reuniao/$token` (service role, always by token) or the CS confirms in the client page; the chosen slot becomes `agendada`, the others are deleted, a timeline entry is added and the owner gets a bell notification. `reservada` never counts as a held meeting.
- When a client picks an offered slot (link or CS confirmation), `avisarEscolha` notifies the offer owner, the client's `cs_responsavel_id` and `comercial_responsavel_id` by bell and e-mail (`enviarMencaoPorEmail` with a custom `rodape`). The commercial agenda (`getComercialAgenda`) also lists `cs_meetings` owned by the executive (`origem: "cliente"`, `reservado` for offered slots).
- After an offered slot is chosen, `enviarConviteDaReuniaoCs` (`src/lib/cs/convite-reuniao.server.ts`) e-mails a calendar invite (.ics METHOD:REQUEST + "Adicionar à Google Agenda" link) to the client contact and the offer owner, reusing `buildIcs`/`sendEmail` from the SDR meeting invite. The CRM does not write to Google Calendar directly (integration is read-only iCal).
- Google Calendar write (per-user OAuth): `src/lib/google/agenda.ts` (pure: auth URL, event body) and `agenda.server.ts` (token exchange/refresh, insert/patch/delete with `sendUpdates=all` and Meet). Tokens live in `google_agenda_contas` (RLS on, no policies: service role only); OAuth state in `google_agenda_estados`; callback `/api/public/google/callback`; secrets `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`/`GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`. CS meetings sync via `sincronizarReuniaoCsNoGoogle` (agendada → create/update, cancelada → cancel, delete → cancel first), storing `cs_meetings.google_event_id`/`google_event_dono`; agendas hide the iCal copy of Compass-created events (iCal UID = `<eventId>@google.com`). When the owner is connected, offers skip the .ics e-mail (Google invites the client).
- "Tudo é contato": every `contacts` row of a CS client's company is also a `cs_contacts` row (origin `empresa`), via `cs_trazer_pessoas_da_empresa()` and triggers on `contacts` (insert/company change) and `cs_clients` (insert/company change). They never get `analista_pda` automatically, and the journey e-mail recipient picker skips origin `empresa` unless analista PDA. The "Jornada do cliente" item was removed from the menu (route `/jornada` still exists).
- Ficha única por empresa: /empresas/$uid aceita o código da ficha do CS, uma ficha mesclada (redireciona) ou o id da empresa (abre a ficha dela ou, se ainda não é cliente, a página da empresa). A ficha mostra também negócios, propostas, leads, faturas e requerimentos (src/lib/empresa-unificada.functions.ts). Mesclar empresas junta as fichas do CS (mesclar_clientes_cs, cs_clients.mesclado_em; o sync do RD segue mesclado_em).
- Empresas, contatos e negócios são de todos os times (menu e rotas abertos; leitura das fichas do CS para quem tem time: pode_ver_empresas). Empresas lista também a base sem ficha (empresas_da_base, filtro "Empresas"); /contatos pagina no servidor (src/lib/contatos.functions.ts). Visões salvas ficam no banco por pessoa (visoes_salvas, useSavedViews(objeto, padrão)): renomear, salvar filtros, duplicar, mover, excluir, restaurar padrão.
- Dia 1 do CS: cs_settings.inicio_operacao ({data}) — prazos (etapa, sem contato, cadência) contam a partir dele (src/lib/cs/inicio-operacao.ts `ancorar`), e o que venceu antes não aparece como atrasado nas Tarefas nem nas escalações (cs_inicio_operacao() em cs_escalations). Editável em Configurações CS → SLA. Registros de teste apagados em 27/11 estão em lixeira_testes (jsonb, só service role).
- Negócios comerciais novos do RD ([C] Vendas, parceiros, cross/up sell, indicação): `trazerNegociosNovosDoRd` (src/lib/rd/rd-negocios-novos.server.ts, hook POST /api/public/hooks/rd-negocios-novos, pg_cron) lê os negócios CRIADOS nos últimos dias (a API do RD não filtra por alteração) e cria só os que faltam via `transformRdToObjects({ filtro, soCriar: true })` — o que já existe no Compass não é tocado. A migração de 08/11 foi a única carga completa desses funis; o sync de 15 min (`runRdSync`) é só dos funis do SDR.
