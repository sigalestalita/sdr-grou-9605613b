# AGENTS.md

- Proposal slide images live in the private `propostas-slides` bucket (workspace blocks public buckets); `conteudo.apresentacao.slides` stores public-format URLs and the public page swaps them for signed URLs server-side, always looked up by token.
- Proposal visits (`proposta_visitas`) are written only by server functions in `propostas-publico.functions.ts` with the service role; one row per browser session, and preview (`?previa=1`, `?imprimir=1` or a signed-in Compass user) records nothing.
- Proposal alert e-mails (`src/lib/propostas/avisos-email*.ts`) go through the same Resend connector as the other Compass e-mails, to `propostas.avisos_emails` (empty → the sender's profile e-mail); they never throw, every attempt is logged in `integration_logs` (action `proposta_aviso_email`), `PROPOSTA_AVISOS_EMAIL_SIMULAR=1` skips the real send, and a signed-in Compass caller (preview) gets no e-mail.
- Site forms enter through `POST /api/public/mkt/conversao` (rules in `src/lib/mkt/conversao.ts`, writes in `conversao.server.ts`, service role only): the asset's public `chave` authenticates, every submit is a new immutable `mkt_conversoes` row, and the SDR card is created/updated in `leads` with the lead/contact spine linked by the existing `leads_vincula` trigger.
- `leads.sem_ia_automatica` (copied from `mkt_ativos.sem_ia_automatica`) must be honored by every automatic WhatsApp sender for new leads: AI opening, `new_lead`/`stage_change`/`no_reply` rules and FUP sequences; routing and the human SDR cadence still apply.
- New users: `createUser` sends no e-mail; the super admin edits and sends the welcome e-mail right after (or later via "Boas-vindas" in Usuários) through `sendWelcomeEmail` (draft/HTML in `src/lib/boas-vindas.ts`, Resend, logged in `integration_logs` action `boas_vindas_email`). It links to the public manual at `/manual` (`src/content/manual-compass.html`, served without login by `src/routes/manual.ts`).
- Usage logs: `useRegistroDeUso` (in the `_app` shell) records each tab session, page opened and a 5-minute "active" ping (only with the tab visible and recent interaction) into `uso_eventos` via `registrarUso` (service role; RLS on, no policies). Only the e-mails in `ACESSO_LOGS_USO` (`src/lib/uso.ts`, today just talita.sigales@grougp.com.br) can read them, at `/uso` (summaries come from the `uso_resumo`/`uso_telas`/`uso_ultimo_acesso` SQL functions, service role only). Events before the app started logging (from 23/11/2026) were reconstructed from login refreshes and recorded CRM actions, flagged `reconstruido = true` (tipos `aberto`/`acao`, deduped by `origem_id`); `aberto` never counts as active time.
- E-mail marketing segments come from `email_segmento_leads(regras)`: `regras.lista = <segment id>` makes it a pasted/uploaded list (`email_segmento_membros`, imported by `importarListaDeEmails`, parsed by `src/lib/email-marketing/lista.ts`; other rules are ignored, unsubscribes still excluded); `regras.carteiras` matches an executive's portfolio (cs_clients.comercial_responsavel_id, companies.owner_user_id or leads.rd_owner = profile name), unlike `responsaveis` (leads.assigned_to).
- Executives open deals themselves with "Novo negócio" (`criarNegocio` in `comercial-negocio.functions.ts`, dialog in `src/components/comercial/novo-negocio.tsx`): reuses the company by exact name and the contact by e-mail, same shape as `passar_lead_para_executivo`; only managers create for someone else. `deals.reuniao_em` is editable on the deal ("Reunião marcada") and the Início "Reuniões hoje" merges SDR leads, the executive's deals and Google Agenda events with external invitees.
- WhatsApp campaign filter `carteiras` resolves lead ids with `leads_da_carteira(uuid[])` (same portfolio rule as e-mail `regras.carteiras`), then applies the other filters in batches of 300 ids (`leadsDaAudiencia`).
- CS meetings: a scheduled meeting is a `cs_meetings` row with status `agendada` (any number per day) and optional `contact_id` (→ `cs_contacts`), created from the client page ("Agendar reunião") or the CS agenda (`AgendarReuniaoDialog`). `listCsAgenda` merges these with Google events; Google events can be linked to a client/contact by hand (`vincularEventoDaAgenda`, service role after an RLS read check). Editing a meeting updates its single timeline entry (matched by `metadata.meeting_id`) and keeps the owner. The CS dashboard counts only realized meetings as held.
- Client contacts (`cs_contacts`) have their own `tags text[]` (besides company tags in `cs_client_tags`), edited with `tagDoContato`; a realized masterclass meeting tags its contact too. The company page (`getCsClient`) hides contacts and "outras pessoas" without e-mail (they stay in the database; `semEmailOcultos` reports how many).
- Company merge (`/empresas-parecidas`, managers only): `empresas_parecidas()` groups active companies by same CNPJ, same domain or `empresa_chave(nome)` (no accents/punctuation/legal suffixes), minus `empresas_grupos_ignorados`; `mesclar_empresas(fica, sai)` repoints every FK to `companies(id)` (discovered from pg_constraint), fills the survivor's empty fields, logs `empresas_mescladas` (incl. `sai_rd_org_id`) and deletes the merged one. The RD transform maps a merged company's `rd_org_id` to the survivor so it is not recreated.
- PDA analysts: `analistas_pda` (unique by lower(email)) is filled by the CSV import in Configurações CS (`lerAnalistasPda` → `importarAnalistasPda` → SQL `importar_analistas_pda`, managers only), which links the company by CNPJ, existing contact e-mail, unique corporate domain or unique `empresa_chave` name (else "sem empresa", linkable by hand with vinculo `manual`, never overwritten by re-imports) and flags `contacts`/`cs_contacts.analista_pda`. The company page tab "Analistas PDA" (`analistasDaEmpresa`) lists them plus `cs_contacts` flagged as analysts.
- Company review (`/empresas-parecidas`): each group gets a manager decision — merge, "São filiais" (`ligar_filiais`: sets `companies.parent_company_id` to a mother company from the group, an existing one or a new one, mirrors `cs_clients.parent_client_uid` when both are CS clients, logs `empresas_grupos_ignorados.decisao = 'filiais'` with `mae_id`/`filhas`) or "não são a mesma" (`decisao = 'diferentes'`); groups whose members already share one parent are hidden, so a new unlinked unit brings the group back. "Revisadas" lists decisions and merges; `reabrir_grupo_de_parecidas` undoes a decision (unlinks the filiais). `pareceFiliais` (src/lib/empresas-parecidas.ts) flags branch-like groups (unit codes, UF, "regional", same domain with different names).
